# AlibiMe support

AlibiMe calls or texts you at a time you choose, so you have a reason to
leave. If something doesn't work, write to {support_email} and say what you
tried and roughly when. We answer as soon as we can.

## The call or text didn't arrive

Check that the number in Settings is yours and that Do Not Disturb or a Focus
isn't silencing calls from unknown numbers. History shows what happened to
each call and text.

## Credits

A call uses more credits than a text; Add Credits in Settings shows how many.
If a call or text can't be sent, or you cancel it before it goes out, you get
the credits back. Once a call has gone out it counts, even if you don't pick
up.

## Refunds

Purchases go through Apple, so Apple handles refunds: open
[reportaproblem.apple.com](https://reportaproblem.apple.com) and pick the
purchase. Credits from a refunded purchase are taken off your balance.

## Deleting your account

Settings, Delete account removes your number, history, own alibis and any
credits left, right away. Credits you bought are not refunded.
